oathon verify-bundle ▌

Independent, in-browser verification of Oathon evidence bundles.
Your bundle never leaves this browser. All verification runs locally (WebCrypto); this page makes zero network calls.
Drop an evidence bundle here — a bundle folder or .zip — or click to choose a folder.

What a green result means — and what it does not: Oathon proves the integrity of evidence after durable recording, under the stated conditions (see SECURITY.md in the specification). It does not prove that the recording runtime told the truth before recording, that the runtime was uncompromised, or anything about legal admissibility. RFC 3161 receipts attest that a digest existed by the TSA's timestamp under that TSA's certificate policy; receipt (DER/CMS) verification is NOT COVERED by this page — use the SDK's oathon verify-bundle for that. Wire identifiers remain WARRANT-*-V0.1 per the compatibility note in CRYPTOGRAPHY.md §5.
